# Governance

Cluster API Provider for Proxmox VE (CAPMOX) is an open source project licensed
under Apache-2.0 and follows the
[CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

## Roles

**Contributors** are everyone who opens issues or pull requests, reviews, or
helps users. No sign-up is needed beyond the
[contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Maintainers** are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) and
[CODEOWNERS](CODEOWNERS). They review and merge pull requests, approve CI runs
for external contributions, cut releases, triage security reports and own the
project roadmap.

## Decision making

Day-to-day decisions are made by lazy consensus on issues and pull requests: a
proposal is accepted when a maintainer approves it and no maintainer objects
within 7 days. A pull request needs at least one approving review from a
maintainer who is not its author.

When consensus cannot be reached, maintainers vote. A decision passes with a
simple majority of all maintainers, each maintainer having one vote. Votes are
held in a GitHub issue and stay open for at least 7 days.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor with a sustained record of quality contributions and reviews may
be nominated by an existing maintainer in a GitHub issue. The nomination is
accepted by a simple majority of maintainers. Maintainers are expected to act
in the interest of the project and not only of their employer; the project
welcomes maintainers from several organisations.

## Stepping down and removal

A maintainer may step down at any time by opening a pull request that removes
them from `MAINTAINERS.md` and `CODEOWNERS`. A maintainer who has been
inactive for 6 months, or who repeatedly violates the Code of Conduct, may be
removed by a simple majority of the other maintainers.

## Changing this document

Changes to governance are made by pull request and require approval from a
simple majority of maintainers.
